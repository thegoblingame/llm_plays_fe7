# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.
- Once Merlinus has joined your party, you will be prompted at the beginning of every single chapter whether or not you want to deploy him. Always select yes.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- (s01) On a Defend chapter, build the choke two layers deep. Every tile within 2 of the protected unit must be occupied or unreachable even if the front blocker dies. One dead blocker plus one empty tile behind it lost Ch.29. Also, count magic damage against a low-Res blocker: three mage hits at about 11 each kills a 31-HP fighter.

### Chapter 29 — Cog of Destiny

`attempts: 1`

**Objectives:**

Defend Nils for 11 turns (on-screen: "Defend Nils 1/11 Turn").

**Recruits:**

| character | starts as | recruit with / action | important condition                          |
| --------- | --------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **vaida** | enemy     | **hector**            | she must have survived as an enemy in ch. 26 |

**Agent notes:**

**s01: LOST on the turn 9 enemy phase. Nils died, so it was GAME OVER.** The screen shows GAME OVER. No state was saved; the last good state is the human's copy from the start of this chapter. The playthrough began here, not at the Prologue. I filed this under Ch.29 because the map is a throne room with green knights and the objective is "Defend Nils, 11 turns", but that identification is my inference.
- Map: Nils (green, 10 HP) sits at (10,1) in a sealed throne room. The only way in is the 2-wide stairs (10,5)/(11,5) → (10,4)/(11,4); the Brace tiles (3F) are impassable. The enemy floods up from the south with about 50 units: mages, cls1C light casters, archers, knights and soldiers with Axereaver, fighters with Swordreaper, and mercenaries with Lancereaver. Reinforcements spawn at the map edges every turn.
- What worked (turns 1-8): two blockers on (10,4)/(11,4). Each player phase, Lucius stood on (10,3) or (11,3) and finished wounded enemies on the stairs with 100%-hit, no-counter magic. Blockers rotated every turn, and Priscilla's staff (4B) heals 12-24. About 17 kills, no losses.
- What lost it: I left (10,3) empty behind Bartre on (10,4). Bartre (31/41 HP, only 3 Res) was hit by three attackers, most likely magic (inferred), and died. An enemy then walked through to (10,3), which is range 2 from Nils, and killed him. **Always keep BOTH (10,3) and (11,3) occupied.** Then nothing can get within 2 tiles of Nils even if a blocker falls. Use high-Res units on the blockers (Marcus has 12 Res, #6/cls33 has 8), not Bartre.
- Deaths: Bartre (on the turn Nils died). Vaida never appeared by turn 9, and she cannot be recruited anyway because Talk is not supported.
- Items: Isadora's item 5A was a permanent +7 max HP (28→35). Marcus's iron axe and hand axe are gone. #6's javelin is down to about 2 uses. Raven picked up an Armorslayer.
