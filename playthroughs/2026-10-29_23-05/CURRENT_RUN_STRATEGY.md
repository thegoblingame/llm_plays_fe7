# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- (s01) Lyn and Eliwood are game-over units in Hector mode, but Pick Units lets you bench them; do that on hard chapters unless the plot forces them in.
- (s01) Prep > Trade: Merlinus offers Sell (and convoy transfer), so gems and loot can be sold before battle without a shop. Shop menus eat the first input after dialogue: wait ~60 frames and screenshot before moving the cursor.
- (s01) Rescue = invulnerability: carried units cannot be targeted. On defend/survive maps, have tanks carry the soft units for the final enemy phases.

### Chapter 18 — Pirate Ship

**Objectives:**

Survive 11 turns (chapter ends after the turn-11 enemy phase). Survive (Hector mode) — Lyn, Eliwood and Hector deaths are all game over.

**Recruits:**

None.

**Agent notes:**

s01 attempt 1 (LOST on the turn-11 enemy phase — Lyn died = GAME OVER; Lyn is a game-over unit in Hector mode, like Eliwood/Hector):
- Army is badly underleveled (Eliwood Lv3, Rebecca Lv2, Guy/Bartre/Matthew Lv4), no healer, convoy EMPTY, 0 gold at start.
- Armory = green cabin door on our ship's middle deck. Lyn and Oswin each hold a Red Gem (sells 2500G). Sold Lyn's; bought Steel axe + Hand axe (Hector), Javelin + Steel lance (Oswin).
- Waves: t1 pegasi (17HP, Def4, weak) from the south + mercs/archer on the east ship; shamans from the west ship over the west plank; t7 a ship docks at our STERN (mercs + shaman); t9-10 ~5 more pegasi arrive from the south. Enemies otherwise passive.
- Marcus/Oswin/Hector take 0 from mercs and pegasi; shamans (Flux 10) are the real threat to Hector. Canas (Res 8) is the anti-shaman unit.
- Rescue is great here: carried units can't be targeted (Marcus carried Eliwood, Sain carried 7HP Kent).
- What killed us: on the last enemy phase Lyn (22HP, Def3) was left with open sides near the pegasi. Next time CARRY Lyn too (Oswin/Hector/Marcus can rescue her) or wall her completely on turns 10-11.

s01 attempt 2 — WON (survived all 11 turns), 0 deaths, nobody to recruit.
- Benched Lyn and Eliwood (Pick Units allows it; both are game-over units) and Matthew; fielded Hector, Marcus, Oswin, Sain, Kent, Bartre, Guy, Erk, Canas, Rebecca, Wil (+ Merlinus).
- Money: in Prep > Trade, Merlinus's "Sell" sells items with no shop. Lyn, Matthew and Florina each had a Red Gem (2500G each), and the archer drops one more. Ended with ~9600G banked, which is plenty for future shops.
- Marcus (Iron sword, 20 dmg, 100% hit) did most of the work: shamans, mercs and pegasi die to him and none of them can hurt him. Sain's Steel lance one-shots shamans (19 dmg).
- Turns 9-11: tanks Rescue the soft units (Oswin+Wil, Bartre+Erk, Kent+Guy, Hector+Rebecca, Sain+Canas) and wait in the bow, and the pegasus wave does almost nothing. Kent dropped to 9 HP against an Armorslayer mercenary on the last enemy phase; give the carriers a little more buffer.
- Bought Hector a Steel axe + Hand axe (sold his Devil axe) and an Iron sword for Marcus. Sain holds a Guiding Ring (now given to Canas, Lv9; usable at Lv10). Canas read his Secret Book (+Skl).
- Handover: everyone alive. Kent ~9 HP and Sain ~17 HP at the end, but HP resets between chapters. Ch19 Prep auto-deployed 12, including Eliwood. Lyn's level is 10 and Eliwood is still Lv3, so keep both lords out of danger or bench them when possible.
