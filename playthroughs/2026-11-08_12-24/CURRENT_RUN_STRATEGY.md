# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- (s01) Survive/choke chapters: a plug that can COUNTER-KILL invites refills (each kill frees the doorway tile for a fresh attacker the same phase). Trading away the plug's weapons turns the enemy on the doorway into a harmless permanent cork. Check enemy weapon IDs with fe7_state: 0x44 = Flux (dark magic, targets Res), 0x0E = Armorslayer.
- (s01) Merlinus (cls44, ch28) cannot be selected or moved; selecting his tile opens the field menu. Raw button presses QUEUE: after a suspend, wait for each screen with a screenshot before the next press, or a stray A will hit Resume Chapter.

### Chapter 18 — Pirate Ship

**Objectives:**

Survive 11 turns. Losing Hector (or Eliwood) is a game over.

**Recruits:**

None.

**Agent notes:**

s01 attempt 1 (LOST, turn 6, Hector died): you start in the upper cabin (rows 0-3, x7-12). It has only two entrances, (8,4) west and (11,4) east, both reached from row 5. Hand axes (range 2) at (8,5)/(9,5)/(8,6) all hit (8,4), and tiles in row 3 are hit through the doorways, so stay in rows 0-2. Holding (8,3) instead of (8,4) cuts it to one melee attacker plus one range-2 attacker. No healer was deployed and only one vulnerary. Pirates doubled the low-Def units (Def 4) for about 20, which killed #7. Do NOT plug with Hector: I put him on (8,3) at 26 HP because the forecasts showed 1-3 damage per hit, yet he died in one enemy phase (the cls0E units carry 0E, probably Killing Edges, so crits). The east plug Oswin/cls14 (Def 14) also dropped from 22 to 9. Next try: deploy a healer if prep allows, plug with the highest-Def units only, keep Hector and Eliwood at the back, and rotate before HP drops under ~60%.

s01 attempt 2 (LOST, turn 7, Hector died again): the (8,3) and (11,3) formation worked. #1 swordsman (ch1A, Def 12) on (8,3) and the knight (ch0B, Def 14) on (11,3) held turns 1-5 with no losses, and #1 killed about 9 enemies on the enemy phase of turn 5. The enemy phase of turn 6 is the surge: both plugs lose 10-20 HP. After it, about 11-13 enemies remain. Mistakes: I left the knight in at 12 HP (he died), and I plugged with Hector at 16 HP. He dies every time despite forecasts of 1-3 damage per hit. NEVER put Hector at a choke. For turns 6-7, rotate in #2 (ch19), #4 or #7 (30+ HP) instead, kill anything that gets inside (archers can't counter at melee), and use Ninian to refresh attackers. (Correction: #8 ch28 is MERLINUS' tent, not Ninian. It can't dance and should stay in a safe corner.)

s01 attempt 3 (deliberately restarted on turn 6 after #2 and Merlinus died): KEY FACT: the cls24 enemies carry item 0x44 = FLUX (dark magic, range 1-2, about 12 Atk vs RES). They are shamans, not axe fighters. Physical Def is irrelevant against them; Res is what counts. Res: #1 ch1A swordsman 9, knight ch0B 4, Hector 2, Eliwood 1, mage 1, #2/#4/#7 0. The cls0E units carry 0x0E = Armorslayer (effective vs the knight). Shamans have Def 2-3, about 18 HP and AS 2-3, so every melee unit doubles them, and Eliwood's Rapier does 13x2 at 100% hit. Plan: #1 is the only real plug. The cabin can't be held passively against Flux, so kill the shamans on the player phase and keep low-Res units out of their 1-2 range. With more units' Res, the turn 5-6 surge kills plugs.

s01 attempt 4 (deliberately restarted on turn 10 after #1, the mage and #7 died; Hector was about to be forced into the plug): Merlinus (#8) CANNOT be selected or moved at all. The convoy holds only 2 weapons. The Flux damage is lower than I feared: about 6 vs the knight, 10 vs Hector, 11-12 vs Res-0 units. What actually kills is REFILLS. Every enemy a plug counter-kills on the doorway tile (8,4)/(11,4) lets a fresh one step in and attack the same phase, so #1 died on turn 7 after counter-killing about 11. Timeline: turns 1-4 quiet; turn 5-7 phases are the surge (around 25 enemies); reinforcements spawn at the south edge on turns 8-10, so it never thins out. Lessons: (1) Leave crippled enemies alive on the doorway tiles as corks, and avoid plugs whose counter kills them. (2) On the player phase, kill shamans that stand ON (8,4)/(11,4) only when you can also re-plug. (3) Rotation candidates in order: #1 (Res 9), the knight (Def 15, Res 4, but never east once Armorslayers are near, from about turn 5), Hector (only east, only at full HP, one phase max), then #4/#7 (30+ HP). (4) Eliwood's Rapier kills shamans at 100% (14x2).

**s01 attempt 5: WON (survived through the enemy phase of turn 11; the chapter does NOT end at the start of turn 11). No deaths, no recruits available.** THE WINNING TRICK: DISARMED PLUGS. Trade away every weapon a plug holds. A plug that cannot counter never kills the enemy standing on the doorway tile, so a full-HP pirate parks there as a permanent cork (pirates do 0-2 to Def 12-15) and the doorway never refills. Each door then faces only ONE ranged Flux tile ((8,5) west, (11,5) east). #1 (ch1A, Res 9) on (8,3) took about 3 per phase all chapter. East: the knight took about 7 per phase (vulnerary on turn 4), then disarmed Hector (turns 8-10, about 10 per phase), then disarmed #7 on turn 11.
HANDOVER, weapons are scattered and MUST be traded back at the start of the next chapter: #1 (ch1A) holds NOTHING (his 05 sword is with Eliwood, his 01 sword with #4/ch09). The knight (ch0B) holds 26 and Hector's 1F axe; his lance 0x14 is with the mage (ch0E). The mage also holds Hector's 8D (Wolf Beil?) and #7's (ch08) axe 0x20. Hector holds nothing. #7 holds only item 5B. Low HP at the end of the chapter: Hector 5, knight 5, #1 13 (chapter transition likely refills HP). The vulnerary was used up.
