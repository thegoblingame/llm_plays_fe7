# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- If an objective is unclear, actually commit Wait on special tiles (gates especially): some triggers only fire on Wait, and the menu looks ordinary beforehand. Backing out proves nothing.
- Sprites stand taller than their tile, so check the unit panel before pressing `A`. `A` on an empty tile opens the field menu, and blind follow-up presses can land on Suspend. L also cycles to Merlinus, who can't act.
- Lyn and Eliwood are on the field as lords: either one dying is an instant GAME OVER. Read their HP at Preparations (only 23 and 21 in Ch. 26) and keep them out of first-turn reach. A lord carried by Hector cannot be targeted.
- In the forecast, the "x2" marker covers the last digit of Mt ("2x2" can mean 28, hit twice). After a Dance, send the cursor move in a separate press call, or it gets swallowed and the next A opens the field menu. Ninian's Dance also refreshes a ballista rider for a second shot.
- Press only one or two `A`s through mid-phase dialogue: spare `A`s carry into your player phase and open a unit's menu. The turn also ends by itself once every unit but Merlinus has acted, so check the screen before opening the field menu. Staff-capable units get Staff at the top of the menu, not Attack, and it shows only if a wounded ally is in range.
- Green NPCs you must protect can be Rescued if Aid is at least their Con, and a carried unit cannot be targeted. Check the menu next to them. In the Preparations item screens the hand sits left of its option, so read the highlight before A: Give all is right of Trnsfr and empties the unit into the convoy.
- Enemy-phase bait works: a sturdy unit on a Fort or forest counters while Pent and Louise kill from two tiles, where most melee enemies cannot counter. After a kill or level-up the camera shifts, so re-read the cursor before pressing A; blind presses reached End and Suspend in Ch. 29.
- Weapons break mid-chapter and the menu silently drops Attack, so blind `A`s fall into Item and onto Discard. Screenshot the unit menu first. A range 1-2 weapon spends uses on counters at both ranges; a melee axe saves them. Preparations Trade > List can pull items from undeployable units, and a lord may use a Heaven seal at Lv10+ there.
- In shops, press and screenshot per purchase: a cursor press during the "Anything else?" text gets dropped, and the next A buys the wrong item. Backing out of an unused shop undoes the unit's move, so it can scout others.
- Open the Attack list for your main fighters every few turns: weapons run out silently, and a unit with no weapon has no Attack, so a blind `A` chooses the next entry (Take, Item). Empty ballistae look manned; if no Attack appears at 2 tiles, nobody is on them.
- Backing out of a forecast is free, so scout each unit's numbers on a boss before committing anyone. Enemies pick among whoever is in reach, so to force a boss onto your counter, leave only your tank inside its range.
