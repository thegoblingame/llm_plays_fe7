# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- (s01) Black Fang shamans (Flux) shred Res-0 units (fighters/cavaliers/knights take 8-12 per hit); Marcus takes ~3 and is the go-to shaman tank. Hold chokepoints one tile BEHIND the doorway/stair so only ~2 enemies can reach the blocker. There is no healer yet — HP lost is gone for the chapter.
- (s01) Menu hygiene: pressing A on an empty map tile opens the field menu (Unit/Status/Options/Suspend/End); a blind "Up, A" there picks Suspend or End. Screenshot before confirming anything in that menu.

### Chapter 18 — Pirate Ship

**Objectives:**

Survive 11 turns (shown as "Survive 1/11 Turn"). No Preparations screen; starts directly on the map.

**Recruits:**

None.

**Agent notes:**

s01 attempt 1 — LOST turn 9 (Hector killed). Army: Eliwood L9, Hector L8, Dorcas, Bartre, Oswin, Rebecca, Lowen, Marcus, Merlinus. No healer, no vulneraries.
- Map: you start on the upper deck at the top of your ship. Only two stairways (left and right stair-top tiles on the railing row) lead up to it. Enemies arrive over planks from ships to the left/right and flood the lower deck.
- Enemy mix: many Black Fang SHAMANS (Flux, ~10 dmg, range 1-2), swordsmen (myrmidons, ~9-11 dmg, sometimes 100 hit), pirates, an archer. Magic and arrows hit over the railing, so ANY unit on the row just above the railing (row behind the stair-tops) is in range from the lower deck. Rebecca (19 HP) died there; Dorcas died holding the right stair-top (axe vs swords = bad).
- Once one stair fell, enemies poured onto the upper deck and killed everyone in two phases.
- What to do differently: put weapon-triangle-correct tanks on the stair-tops (lance user vs swordsmen: Lowen did well; Marcus is the best tank). Keep everyone else at least 3 tiles from any lower-deck tile. Rotate the blockers BEFORE they get low, and finish wounded enemies with Rebecca from a tile that is safe. Rescuing Hector with Marcus (Aid 14 vs Con 13) works as a last resort.

s01 attempt 2 — LOST in the turn-10 enemy phase (Eliwood killed at 1 HP; Marcus, Dorcas, Rebecca also fell T9-T10). Got much further than attempt 1.
- KEY: block one tile BEHIND each stair-top (the upper-deck tile directly above the stairs), not on the stair-top itself. On the stair-top up to 4 enemies reach you (Bartre lost 26 HP in one phase); on the tile behind only 2 can (one on the stair-top, one ranged from the stair below). Oswin took ~8/phase there. Do this from turn 1.
- Shamans (Flux, 10 dmg even to Hector, 11-12 to fighters/cavaliers) mass on the LEFT stairs; the right side is mostly physical (myrmidons, pirates, fighters, archer). Hector takes 1-4 from physical hits — he is the best right-side blocker; keep him off the left.
- No healing exists, so it is pure attrition: total HP of Oswin/Marcus/Dorcas/Bartre/Lowen must last 10 phases. Rotate BEFORE a blocker drops under ~20. Use Rebecca (range 2) and the blocker to finish wounded stair-top enemies every turn — fewer live enemies = less damage.
- Any ally within 2 tiles of the stair-top is a target. A rescued unit can't be attacked (Dorcas Aid 13 can carry Rebecca, Con 5).

s01 attempt 3 — LOST in the turn-8 enemy phase (Hector killed). Back-tile blocking from turn 1 worked well until turn 7 (only ~0-9 dmg per phase), then the LEFT broke: Dorcas (Res 0) went 25->dead in one phase to shamans, then Oswin, and shamans flooded the deck.
- CRITICAL: Marcus takes only ~3 damage per Flux hit (high Res) and one-rounds shamans (22x2). Fighters (Res 0), Lowen, Hector (10/hit) and Oswin (8/hit) all melt to Flux. => Put MARCUS on the LEFT back tile and keep him there the whole chapter. Use Oswin (Def 14, takes 0-2 from swords) / Hector on the RIGHT (physical side), rotating with Lowen (lance vs swordsmen) — never put fighters on the left.
- Hector does not heal; after his first stint (~10 HP left) keep him out of range for good.

s01 attempt 4 — WON (survived to the end of turn 11; Fargus's ship arrives). Deaths: Lowen + Oswin (T6 enemy phase, right side broke to a swordsman + shaman), Dorcas (T8, left), Rebecca (T11, right). Survivors: Eliwood (Lv10, 20/26), Hector (4/26), Marcus (Lv6, 1 HP), Bartre (18/30), Merlinus. No recruits in this chapter.
- What worked: Marcus on the LEFT back tile T1-T9 (he one-rounds shamans and takes ~2-3 per Flux), killing the stair-top enemy every player phase, and in the last turns sealing Hector in the bow tip (row above Merlinus's tent) with Bartre beside him. Bartre (Aid 12) can rescue Marcus (Con 11).
- What to do better: the RIGHT side also gets a shaman + archer from ~T4; Oswin lost ~10/phase there. Rotate earlier and pair Rebecca's chip damage with the blocker's kill. A flier (pegasus/wyvern) appears over the water on T10-11 — keep lords away from deck tiles it can land next to.
- Note: on T5 a stray field-menu press chose Suspend; I picked Resume Chapter immediately (no rewind, same state).
- Ending: Save prompt -> saved to the slot in use (top). "Deploy Merlinus?" at the next chapter -> Yes (default).
