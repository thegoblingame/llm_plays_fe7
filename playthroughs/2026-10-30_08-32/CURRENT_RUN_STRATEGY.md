# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- Forecast Mt can be misread: the `x2` marker overlaps the second digit, so "2x2" may really be 22x2. Judge a doubtful number by the unit's Atk and the enemy's HP before backing out.
- A session can start with a unit left mid-move (leftover Move): tiles shown, no hover window, `A` on an empty tile does nothing. Press `A` on a highlighted tile to get its menu. The turn may also be well past 1; check Status.
- Check the killer's inventory before a boss kill. With 5 items, the drop opens a list where choosing an item and confirming Yes DISCARDS it for good ("Dropped a Door key"), not a send to the convoy.
- In fog, a Torch lights the area only for that moment, then it fades to a small lasting patch. Screenshot right after using it, and hover anything it reveals.
- When a unit's menu may differ (Rescue or Trade near allies), pressing Up then `A` picks Wait only if the wrap lands there; batched presses twice triggered Rescue/Trade by accident. Screenshot the menu when allies are adjacent. Do not mash `A` through a Visit either: a full inventory sends an item to the convoy.
- Check a unit's forecast before trusting a kill on a Nosferatu user, and hover "enemies" that do not move: tinted forts can look like Knights. Rescue on a mounted unit is the fast way to carry a slow lord to a distant Seize.
- Enemy phases do unseen damage: `A` on an empty tile, then Unit, lists every unit's HP at a glance. Read it at the start of each turn, and a carried low-HP lord can be dropped straight onto the throne. Inputs sent right after a combat are often dropped: screenshot the cursor first.
- Never batch `A`, `Up`, `A` unless a unit is confirmed under the cursor: on an empty tile that sequence opens the field menu and ends the turn. Ninian can Dance a spent healer so they move and heal in the same turn. Check weapon uses in the Item list: Marcus and Guy both ran out mid-chapter, and the first sign is a missing Attack command.
- Preparations "Trade" opens the item screen: Trd, Use, Trnsfr (convoy), List, Sell, Give all. It remembers the last command, so screenshot before pressing `A`, and too many `B`s drop into Pick Units, where `A` benches a unit. Do not press `B` through enemy phases without reading: a death line can pass unseen, so check the roster after each phase. Recruits arrive low level; pull them back to the healer.
- Eliwood's death is GAME OVER, so bench him (and likely Lyn, unconfirmed) when not needed; only Hector is locked in. Using a stat item pops up a message that eats the next inputs: screenshot before pressing on. Enemy bosses may hold until you come close, so a wall plus a bait on a fort beats marching. A Longbow at range 3 gets a free shot at anything without 3-range.
