# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

### Chapter 18 — Pirate Ship

**Objectives:**

Survive 11 turns.

**Recruits:**

None.

**Agent notes:**

**s01 attempt 1 (abandoned on turn 6 after 3 deaths):** You start on a raised top deck (rows 0-3, cols 7-12). The only ways in are the one-tile gaps at (8,4) and (11,4); row 5 below is stairs/shop tiles. About 12 shamans with Flux (and more arriving from the west), plus swordsmen and an archer. Bartre (0 Res; I mislabelled him "Oswin") died to Flux on the front row. Oswin, holding (8,4) at 15 HP, died, and Rebecca died after Oswin fell. Marcus is the MVP: Flux does 2 to him and swords do 0. There is no healer on this deploy; slot #8 is Merlinus (cls44, not selectable). Plan: keep low-Res units at row 2 or behind, plug (8,4) with Marcus, rotate the right plug, and never leave a plug under ~20 HP.

**s01 attempt 2: WON (survived 11 turns; restarted once via Suspend → Restart Chapter after 3 deaths on attempt 1).** Died: Marcus, Bartre, Dorcas. No recruits. Deploy (names from overlay/names.json): Eliwood, Hector, Marcus, Oswin, Bartre, Dorcas, Lowen, Rebecca, Merlinus. Layout: the gaps at (8,4)/(11,4) are the only ways onto the top deck, but a plug ON a gap gets hit from three tiles. Plugging one tile deeper at (8,3)/(11,3) cuts that to two (the gap tile and the tile below it). Flux does about 11-12 to 0-Res units (Bartre died to it), 9 to Rebecca, 2 to Marcus. Swords do 0 to Oswin (Def 15) and Marcus. Marcus and Oswin counter-killed 9 enemies on turn 5 but fell to 1 and 6 HP. Marcus then died at (7,2) after Bartre's deep plug collapsed. Dorcas (9 HP) died as a sacrificial right plug so Hector (11 HP) could hide. Turn 9 reinforcements include 2 pegasus knights (fly over the chokes, weak lances). HP is restored for Ch19, so everyone starts at full. Next time: rotate a plug out at about 15 HP, never at 6, and keep 0-Res units out of every Flux tile. There are no staves in this deploy; bring a healer if prep allows. The game skipped Ch19's prep and title card (fe7_wait's Start presses), so Ch19 begins on turn 1 with no prep.
