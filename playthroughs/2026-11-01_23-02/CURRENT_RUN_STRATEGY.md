# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

**Agent notes:**

s01 attempt 1: LOST on turn 9, enemy phase. Zephiel dying is an instant game over. He took about 6-10 damage per enemy phase from turn 7 (15 HP on T7, 9 on T8, dead T9).
- Map (fog): we start in the NW courtyard. Route I took: east stairs, corridor, south gap, hall, SE stairs, narrow corridor, then west along the south hall to the stairs below Zephiel's walled room. Too slow: Marcus (fastest) was still ~3 tiles from those stairs at the end of T9. I also lost about 2 turns sending Marcus west along the corridor above his room, which does not connect, and back.
- Untested idea: the stairs of the west building next to the start may reach the west hall, then the south hall, faster. Check this on T1.
- Untested idea: Rescue Zephiel with Marcus (Aid 14) to carry him out of danger.
- Hector next to Nino on T9 got Rescue/Item/Wait but NO Talk. Nino wanders in the corridor south of the SE stairs. Try again on a later turn, and try other tiles.
- Dart has only a Torch, Bartre only a Hand axe, Louise a Silver bow with 1 use. No healer or vulneraries seen. Enemy thieves loot the west chests from about T4-6 (one took a Rescue staff).
- Enemies seen: Hammer/Swordslayer fighters, Thunder mage, monk, Divine bishop, thieves, reinforcements around T8.
- Hover a unit before acting: sprites are easy to confuse (I mistook Bartre and Dart for Hawkeye).

s01 attempt 2: LOST on turn 6, enemy phase (Zephiel dead, no Talk or rescue reached). His survival varies by run: T9 on attempt 1, T6 here. "Mother..." is his death line, followed directly by game over.
- Restart Chapter gives a Preparations screen. Roster on the bench: Pent (Physic 3, Elfire), Lyn (Mani Katti; can also recruit Nino), Florina (Iron sword 1 use), Wil, Bartre, Dart. Trade > List shows all owned items and the convoy.
- The game calls this chapter "Ch. 26: Battle before Dawn"; the heading here says 28.
- Eliwood's Heaven Seal shows Use greyed out (Lv12), so he cannot promote now. Speedwings raise Speed, not Move.
- Rescue is offered on green units (Marcus got it next to Nino), so Zephiel can likely be carried.
- Fastest Marcus timeline found: T1 past east stairs, T2 south gap (stair is out of range), T3 must kill the Swordslayer fighter blocking the stair, T4 SE stairs, T5 next to Nino's corridor, T6 south hall at the room's level. Still ~T8 to reach him: too slow to rely on.
- Room entrance: wide stairs on the south side of his walled room, reached from the south hall by going west past the brick building. The stairs south of the corridor above the room do not connect.

s01 attempt 3: LOST on turn 13, enemy phase, two turns short. THE RESCUE WORKS: Marcus reached Zephiel on T8 and carried him T8-T12 while Zephiel took no damage. Marcus then died (T12 enemy phase), which dropped Zephiel, and he died the next phase.
- Marcus timeline that worked: T1 past east stairs (use Speedwings, then Canto 1 more tile), T2 south gap, T3 kill the Swordslayer on the stair, T4 SE stairs, T5 next to Nino, T6 south hall, T7 room entrance, T8 tile south of Zephiel > Rescue > Canto into the SE corner of the room.
- Marcus took 13-25 damage per phase in that corner. Pent's Physic reached him from the corridor north of the room, 8 tiles away through the wall, and healed ~29 each. All 3 uses went (one was wasted on Rebecca).
- What ran out: Marcus's Steel lance and Iron sword broke, leaving Slim lance and Iron axe. Hawkeye's weapon broke by T11, leaving him useless. Give both spare weapons at Preparations, and give Marcus Vulneraries.
- Fog: enemies out of a unit's own vision do not appear on its menu until vision updates. Staying a tile further back cost me nothing, but it blocked an archer shot.
- Hector still had no Talk next to Nino on T12. Try Lyn. Florina cannot cross the south wall; only the moat is flyable, and it did not lead anywhere useful.

s01 attempt 4 (in progress when written): LYN recruits Nino: Talk appeared for Lyn on T9 from the tile south of her in the corridor below the SE stairs. Hector never got Talk here, despite the Recruits table. Rebecca died on T3's or T4's enemy phase after I left her on the south gap stair with a Thunder mage in reach; keep archers back from that stair until the mage is dead.

s01 attempt 4 result: LOST on T14 enemy phase, because LYN DIED AND HER DEATH IS A GAME OVER. Marcus was still alive at 8 HP or less carrying Zephiel. Lyn stood idle in the corridor south of the SE stairs from T10 on; reinforcements come up there. After recruiting Nino, send Lyn back north at once and keep her moving away. Hawkeye (T13) and Jaffar (T14) also died that attempt.
- What worked: Pent in the corridor north of the room reaches Marcus with Physic, used T9, T10, T11. Marcus at the room's NE corner (moved there T13) has only two open neighbours and took less damage than the SE corner.
- For next time: put convoy Vulneraries on Marcus at Preparations (he only needs Speedwings, one lance, one sword). Physic runs out by T11, so he needs T12-T15 healing from items. Hector and Oswin were idle: use them as blockers on Marcus's open tiles if they can get in.
