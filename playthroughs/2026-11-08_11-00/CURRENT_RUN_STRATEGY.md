# Playthrough Strategy

Pre-made strategy for the agent, plus the agent's own notes added as it plays. Every chapter has
its own section below with a **Recruits** table (pre-filled by the human) and an **Agent notes**
area (filled in by the agent). Broad, cross-chapter lessons go under **Broad strategy notes**.

## General Strategy

- Always select "yes" to accept side missions when offered the choice to do so.

## Recruiting Units

In each chapter, there are often units that appear as green units (uncontrollable allies) or red units (enemies). These units can often be recruited by executing the Talk action with a specific player-controlled unit, but there are some more complex conditions that have to be fulfilled in order to recruit some characters. For each chapter, you have been provided a list of the recruitable units that appear and the conditions that need to be fulfilled in order to recruit the character. You should try to recruit units if possible, but it is not a strict requirement.

The chapter sections below cover Lyn mode and Hector mode. Eliwood mode is not covered. Recruits are the same on normal and hard difficulty throughout the entire game, so use the list for your mode regardless of difficulty.

## Promoting Units

Fire Emblem has a mechanic known as promotion, where a unit advances to a stronger class. A unit must be at least level 10 to be promoted, although it can go as high as level 20 before being promoted. It is up to you to decide when it is appropriate to promote a unit.

In virtually all cases, a unit is promoted by using a specific consumable item that matches its class. For example, a knight can use a knight crest to be promoted to a general.

## Broad strategy notes

Cross-chapter lessons the agent adds as it plays. Append below.

- Menus open on the side of the screen opposite the unit, so the menu's hand pointer can sit next to some other unit; don't let that fool you. The cursor and camera drift often, so hover a unit (name box) before pressing A on it.
- Low-HP enemies that can't damage your blocker make free chokepoint plugs. Black Fang shamans (Flux) are the real threat to Res-0 units.

### Chapter 18 — Pirate Ship

**Objectives:**

Survive 11 turns (game says "Survive 1/11 Turn"). No Preparations screen; starts straight on the map. Hector or Eliwood dying = game over.

**Recruits:**

None.

**Agent notes:**

s01 attempt 1 (LOST, turn 9, Hector killed). Roster: Hector 8, Eliwood 9, Marcus (Paladin, the only strong unit), Oswin 11, Lowen 4, Dorcas 6, Bartre 3, Rebecca 4, Merlinus (tent, no items in convoy; NO healer, NO vulneraries anywhere).
- Map: you start on the top deck of a ship. Enemies arrive by bridges and climb two stairways (left and right of the hatch). Mercenaries and fighters do 0–5 damage to Marcus/Oswin. The killers are ~8+ **shamans (Flux)**: about 8 damage to Oswin, about 13 to anyone with Res 0–2, and only 2 to Marcus (Res 9). One archer climbed up and took Hector from 17 to 4.
- Plugging both stair tops with Oswin + Marcus failed: two shamans focused Oswin (14 HP) and killed him on turn 3, then the deck was swarmed. Lowen (right stair) died on turn 6, Bartre and Dorcas on turn 8, and then everyone else.
- Next time: shamans have about 20 HP and Marcus one-rounds them (23x2), so have Marcus hunt shamans every turn. Never leave a Res-0 unit within 2 tiles of a shaman. Pull wounded units out early, and rescue them (Bartre/Dorcas Aid 12-13 can carry Hector/Eliwood). A low-HP mercenary sitting on a stair is a free blocker, so don't kill it. Consider having Marcus carry Hector from the start (Aid 14 ≥ Con 13) so the lord is never targetable.

s01 attempt 2 (WON at the start of turn 11; Fargus's crew arrives). **Oswin and Bartre died**. Survivors: Hector 26/26, Eliwood ~20/25, Marcus 3/35, Dorcas 12/32 (Lv7), Lowen 17/25, Rebecca 19/20, Merlinus. No healing anywhere, so the next chapter starts with Marcus and Dorcas badly hurt unless HP resets.
- What worked: Marcus held the LEFT stair-top deck tile (the shamans' side; they do only 2 damage to him) and killed one shaman per turn (Iron Sword doubles them). The RIGHT stair (mercenaries/fighters, some with a Lancereaver/Longsword) is where to rotate fresh units in. Leave harmless low-HP mercenaries (0 damage) standing on the stairs as plugs.
- What went wrong: Oswin went down to 2 HP on the right on turn 5 (shamans + archer). I pulled him back to row 2, but he still died on turn 7, as did Bartre (who replaced him and missed twice).
- Turns 10–11: Dorcas rescued Marcus, then gave him to Hector (Aid 12); Lowen rescued Dorcas; Hector and Eliwood plugged the two stairs. Two enemy **pegasus knights** appear on the last turn.
- Ending choice: "Save" → slot 1 (the one in use). The next chapter's "Deploy Merlinus?" → Yes (the default).
