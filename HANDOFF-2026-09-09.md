## 1. The 2026-09-08 enemy-phase freeze — CAUSE UNDETERMINED

Ch.22 turn 4, the board did not move for ~5 m 50 s. **Nobody has explained it yet. This is
open work.** Full evidence in `runs/2026-09-08-stall/` (TIMELINE.md, screenshots, the 12
logged calls, the scripts).

**What it is NOT:**

- Not a hang, a dropped press, or a call that never resolved. Every press reached the
  emulator and every `press_sequence` resolved; `press()` blocks until the bridge reports
  `input_status.pending === 0`. All three `fe7_wait` calls returned normally at ~112 s
  against a 110 s timeout, and `fe7_unstick` completed mid-stall in 1166 ms *with a
  screenshot*. The socket, bridge and input queue were healthy throughout.
- Not the bridge failing to send Start — proved working with the loop's exact parameters.
- Not Start toggling the minimap in a cancelling pair. **Start does not open the minimap
  during the enemy phase** (Grant, confirmed by hand), and this stall was on the enemy
  phase from start to finish.

**Do not repeat either dead theory as fact.** Start the investigation from the established
facts listed in `runs/2026-09-08-stall/TIMELINE.md`.
